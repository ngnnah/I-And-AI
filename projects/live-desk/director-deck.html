Each manager is a session; each worker, a subagent.

Some sites wait on others: the tower needs its foundation.

Every turn, every site: where are we, how did we get here, what's left?

A long thread piles up: work slips, and changes get lost.

Before: private notes the Director couldn't check, rotting as the thread grew.

Now a board at the gate serves both of them.

The Director directs at the exact line, then sends >>.

Add a todo anytime. It stays on the board until done.

A quiet board? A bell nudges the manager.

The end-of-shift whistle: time for a fresh manager.

A tired manager leaves; a fresh one starts from the board.

Too much for one head? Tell the manager to hire a crew.

Before: the Director tracked every change and drafted every message.

Now: tell the managers to message each other.

We all become Directors; the work multiplies, and the city rises.
